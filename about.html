About Dr. Lyubov Yukhymchuk

Evidence-based aesthetic medicine with precision and care

Board-Certified Cosmetic Practitioner

Dr. Lyubov Yukhymchuk is a licensed medical practitioner specializing in cosmetic medicine, aesthetic injectables, and clinical trichology. With over 10 years of experience, her approach combines medical expertise with a conservative, evidence-based methodology.

Every treatment is individualized, focusing on natural results, patient safety, and long-term treatment planning. In addition to clinical practice, Dr. Lyubov Yukhymchuk works as a medical methodologist, developing treatment protocols and contributing to practitioner education in aesthetic medicine.
