Frequently Asked Questions

What should I expect at my first consultation?

Your initial consultation includes a comprehensive medical history review, detailed assessment of your concerns, clinical photography if appropriate, and development of a personalized treatment plan. The consultation typically lasts 45-60 minutes.

Do you require a consultation fee?

Initial consultation fees vary depending on the service. Please contact us for specific pricing. Consultation fees are often applied toward your first treatment.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking consultations 2-3 weeks in advance. Treatment appointments can typically be scheduled 1-2 weeks out, depending on availability and service type.

Do you offer virtual consultations?

Yes, we offer virtual consultations for preliminary assessments and treatment planning discussions. However, an in-person visit is required before any procedures.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Please bring a valid ID, a list of current medications and supplements, any relevant medical records, and a list of questions or concerns you'd like to discuss.

What is your cancellation policy?

We require 48 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Late cancellations or no-shows may incur a fee. Please contact us as soon as possible if you need to change your appointment.
