Clinical Trichology

Comprehensive hair and scalp evaluation with evidence-based treatment protocols addressing hair loss, thinning, and scalp health. Our trichology services combine medical expertise with advanced diagnostic techniques to identify underlying causes and develop effective treatment strategies.

Conditions Treated:

  • Androgenic Alopecia: Pattern hair loss in men and women
  • Telogen Effluvium: Temporary hair shedding and thinning
  • Alopecia Areata: Autoimmune hair loss conditions
  • Scalp Conditions: Seborrheic dermatitis, psoriasis, and other scalp disorders
  • Hair Breakage & Damage: Structural hair issues and fragility
  • Postpartum Hair Loss: Hormonal hair changes after pregnancy

Treatment Protocols:

  • Comprehensive scalp and hair analysis using clinical imaging
  • Medical-grade topical treatments and prescription therapies
  • PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) therapy for hair restoration
  • Microneedling with growth factor serums
  • Nutritional and supplement guidance
  • Lifestyle and hair care protocol optimization
  • Regular monitoring and treatment adjustment
